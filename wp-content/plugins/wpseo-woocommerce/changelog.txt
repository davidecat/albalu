*** Yoast SEO: WooCommerce Changelog ***


2026-09-15 - version 16.9
* add -  Improves Schema generation performance for variable products with multiple images.
* fix -  Fixes a bug where the Schema of product variations showed raw prices not taking into account WooCommerce VAT settings.
* fix -  Fixes a bug where the XML sitemap could fail to generate when products hidden from the catalog were combined with another plugin or theme that also filtered sitemap entries.
* update -  Sets the minimum supported WordPress version to 6.9.
* update -  Bumps the minimum required version of Yoast SEO to 28.5.
* update -  Sets the _WordPress tested up to_ version to 7.1.
